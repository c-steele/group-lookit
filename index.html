A few extra setup tips
  • Keep your baby’s whole face visible. Use soft light from in front, with no dark shadows or glare over their eyes.
  • Your baby’s face should be the lowest and clearest in the camera view. Keep the keyboard out of their reach.
  • Watch your baby, not the movies. Don’t describe the action or try to bring their attention back.
  • You can take a break or stop at any time.
